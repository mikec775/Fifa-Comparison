I analyzed 2,144 players common to FIFA 14 and FIFA 24, specifically those 30 or younger in FIFA 24.

The study aimed to assess how well FIFA 14 age, overall, and potential ratings predicted FIFA 24 overall ratings. 

Baseline assumptions showed a substantial mean absolute error (MAE) of about 8 points, while using FIFA’s potential rating improved the prediction to an MAE of 5. 

Three machine learning models (linear regression, random forest, and gradient boosting) further reduced the MAE to approximately 3.7 to 3.8 points with an R² of 0.32 to 0.33, indicating a linear relationship. Key findings included regression to the mean and the importance of Potential_14 as a feature. 


Overall, FIFA 14 ratings were moderately informative, explaining about one-third of the variation in FIFA 24 ratings, with other factors like injuries and transfers contributing to remaining discrepancies.
